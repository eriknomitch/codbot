# Multiplayer Overview: Movement and Gunplay

[← Back to index](../README.md)

## The big idea

Infinity Ward calls MW4 multiplayer "slick but grounded." It's faster than MW3 (2023), but slower than the Black Ops games. There is **no Omnimovement** (the dive-in-any-direction system from Black Ops 6/7).

## Classic features that came back

- Red dots on the minimap (enemies show when they fire unsuppressed)
- Map voting
- Theater mode
- "Pick 3" perk system
- Classic Prestige (optional)

## Movement: what's new

| Move | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mantle Slide** | Go over low cover (a car hood, a planter) straight into a slide |
| **Mantle Momentum** | You keep your speed while climbing over things. No hard stop. |
| **Mantle Steering** | You can turn, aim, or cancel while climbing |
| **Supine Slide** | Double-tap crouch while sprinting. You slide lower and end up on your back, feet first. |
| **Pipe Climb / Fast Pipe Slide** | Climb up or slide down pipes on buildings |
| **Hang Shimmy** | Move left or right while hanging from a ledge |
| **Hang Lean** | Aim and turn 360° while hanging |
| **Climbing Jump** | Jump in any direction from a ledge, ladder, or pipe |
| **Slide canceling** | Yes, it's in the game |

**Tactical Sprint** now slows down as you get tired, instead of a fixed burst.

**Practice:** the **Proving Grounds** is a movement training course with tutorials and timed runs (aim for S rank).

These moves work in Campaign, Multiplayer, DMZ, and Warzone.

## Gunplay: "Ballistic Authority"

This is the name for the new gun system. In simple terms:

- **No bloom.** Hipfire bullets go exactly where the gun points. No random spread. (Sway, recoil, and movement still matter.)
- **Same field of view for gun and world.** The gun on screen looks more natural and realistic.
- **Enhanced FOV (PC only).** You see more without enemies looking smaller.
- **Muzzle smoke is masked** around your aim point, so you can still see your target.
- **Better depth of field.** Your aim point stays sharp when aiming.
- **Laser sights match where bullets go.**

## Loadout changes

- **Create-a-Class** is a simple vertical list again.
- Each class now has **its own Operator and its own Killstreaks**.
- **Gunny** is a helper that builds a gun for you (Short Range, Balanced, or Long Range) from attachments you've unlocked.
- **Attachments unlock once, for all guns.** Unlock a scope on one rifle and it works on every gun that can use it.
- **Apex Attachments** are special bonus attachments you get when you max out a gun. They don't use an attachment slot and they change how the gun works (see [04-weapons.md](04-weapons.md)).
- **Smartstreaks:** killstreaks now fill from kills *and* playing the objective. Kills still count the most.

## Kill Block (the changing map)

- Official name: the **Westbridge Training Facility**.
- Built from **3 sections** (two outer, one middle) that change between rounds.
- **500+ combinations** at launch. Some sections look like classic maps: **Crash, Highrise, Shoot House**.
- Weather can change too: clear, rain, or snow.
- Same size as the old Shoot House map.
- At launch it hosts **3v3 and 10v10 Gunfight**. More modes are planned (Season 1 adds a fixed "Crash" version with other modes).
