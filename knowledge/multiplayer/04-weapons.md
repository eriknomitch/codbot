# Weapons (Launch Arsenal)

[← Back to index](../README.md)

**33 weapons at launch:** 24 primary, 8 secondary, 1 melee. They're shared across Multiplayer, DMZ, and (from Season 1) Warzone.

**Key terms:**
- **Unlock level** = the player level you need before you can use the gun in Create-a-Class (CaC unlocks at level 4).
- **Max level** = weapon levels needed to max the gun. Maxing it unlocks its Apex Attachment and lets you Weapon Prestige.
- **Apex Attachment** = a special mod that changes how the gun works. It doesn't use an attachment slot, but it may block some slots.

> Note: a few unlock levels differ between Activision's two launch blogs (for example VEL 46 shows Level 5 in one and 13 in the other). The table uses the weapons blog, which matches wzstats.gg.

## Attachment types

Optic, Muzzle, Barrel, Underbarrel, Magazine, Rear Grip, Stock, Comb, Laser, Ammunition (plus Bolt, Guard, Trigger Action, Cylinder, or Fire Mods on some guns).

**How unlocking works:**
- Each attachment unlocks **once** and then works on every gun that uses it.
- If a weapon level would give you an attachment you already have, you skip that level (Bonus Level Up).
- You can move optics forward or back on the rail.
