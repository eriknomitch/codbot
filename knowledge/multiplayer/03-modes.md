# Multiplayer Modes

[← Back to index](../README.md)

**Hardcore** versions exist for many modes. Hardcore means: small HUD, friendly fire on, less health, and **no health regen**.

## New modes at launch

### Inflation (respawn, 6v6)
- Kill enemies to take their cash.
- The more cash you carry, the bigger target you are. Reach the **High Value Target (HVT)** amount and everyone sees where you are.
- When you die, you drop part of your cash.
- Overtime near the end boosts cash payouts.
- **Most team cash wins.** 10 minutes. Hardcore version available.

### Hijack (round-based objective, 6v6)
- One **Data Spike** in the middle. You can carry it, throw it, or pass it.
- Plant it at the enemy **Comm Station**. It scores points over time, plus a bonus if the upload finishes.
- Defend it after planting, or the enemy can stop it.
- Sides swap each round. 4 minutes a round, 300 points to win.

### Frontline (respawn, 6v6)
- You always spawn at your team's base.
- Kills **outside** your base are worth more, so teams push forward.
- Short spawn protection. Teams swap sides at half score. First to 100 wins.

### Collateral (round-based, 5v5, one life)
- Like **Search & Destroy** plus a **shop**. Everyone starts with a pistol and **$800**.
- Earn cash for kills, plants, defuses, and round wins. Buy gear before each round.
- **No custom loadouts.** If you die, you lose your gear but keep your cash.
- Teams can chip in for a **UAV**.
- First to 6 rounds wins.

### Combat Outpost (Ground War, 32v32)
- Domination, but huge. Capture **Outposts** for points and new spawn spots.
- **Reinforce** captured Outposts to unlock team rewards: Local UAV → JLTV airdrop → **Light Tank** at your HQ.
- 20 minutes, 1,500 points to win.
