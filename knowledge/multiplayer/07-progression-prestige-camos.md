# Progression, Prestige, and Camos

[← Back to index](../README.md)

## Player levels

- **Levels 1–55**, from Private I to **Commander**.
- XP comes from Multiplayer, DMZ, and (from Season 1) Warzone.
- You get at least one unlock every level.
- **DMZ has its own separate track (1–70).** DMZ XP also counts toward 1–55, but Multiplayer XP does **not** count toward DMZ levels.

## Prestige: two paths (new)

When you hit level 55, you choose a path. You choose again at every Prestige.

| | **Classic Prestige** | **Regular Prestige** |
|---|---|---|
| Level reset | Back to 1 | Back to 1 |
| Unlocks and classes | **Reset** (re-earn them) | **Kept** |
| Bonuses | +20% XP, **1 Permanent Unlock Token**, extra cosmetics ("Prime" versions) | Shared rewards only |

- **10 Prestige levels**, then **Prestige Master** from level 56 up to **level 1,000** ("Prestige Legend").
- **Permanent Unlock Token tip:** use it on perks, field upgrades, killstreaks, or equipment, not guns. A gun blueprint from the Store or Battle Pass already keeps that gun unlocked for good.
- Each Prestige honors an older Infinity Ward game (P1 = Call of Duty 2003 ... P10 = MW4).

## Weapon progression and Weapon Prestige

1. Level up a gun → unlock an attachment each level.
2. Max it → get its **Apex Attachment** and the option to **Weapon Prestige**.
3. Weapon Prestige resets the gun to level 1 and relocks attachments, **except** Apex and blueprint attachments. Cosmetics stay.
4. Max it again → **Weapon Prestige Master** (no more resets).

| Camo | How to get |
|---|---|
| Pyroclast | Weapon Prestige 1, Level 1 |
| Pyroclast Terminus | Weapon Prestige 1, max level |
| Pyroclast Scatter | Weapon Prestige Master level 150 |
| Pyroclast Cascade | Weapon Prestige Master level 250 |

## Camo grind (shared across MP, DMZ, and Warzone)

Progress counts in **any mode**. DMZ challenges need higher numbers because AI kills count there.

1. **Military Camos (8)** — earned just by leveling the gun.
2. **Special Camos (3)** — unique per gun (e.g., mounted kills, suppressed kills). Once earned, they go into a shared pool for all guns.
3. **Gold** — all Military and Special done, then e.g. **15 kills in one MP match** (or 100 in one DMZ match).
4. **Platinum** — Gold on every gun in that class, then a class challenge (ARs: headshots; SMGs: kills from behind; and so on).
5. **Harmonic Break** — Platinum on 33 weapons, then a class challenge (ARs: double kills).
6. **Divergence** — complete 33 Harmonic Break challenges.
7. **Mastery Camos** (3 per mode, 9 total):

| Mode | Mastery 1 | Mastery 2 | Mastery 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiplayer | **Mercurial Drift** — 10 kills in a match ×250 | **Polyatomic Reforged** — 3 kills without taking damage ×300 | **Orion Reforged** — 5 kills without dying ×500 |
| DMZ | **Chiral** — 2,500 Tier 3+ enemy kills | **Ripple Tide** — exfil 2 Lieutenant dog tags in one run ×50 | **Helio** — 25,000 kills with Legendary guns on a max-level Operator |
| Warzone (S1) | Parallax | Damascus Reforged | Empyros |

## Other challenges
- **Daily Challenges:** 3 a day, bonus XP for all three, one free reroll a day.
- **Calling Cards:** Multiplayer, DMZ, Shared, and Elite sets. Track up to 9 at once.
- **Reticles:** unlock with ADS kills on each optic; shared across modes.
- **MWII DMZ Beta veterans** get bonus emblems and calling cards on the same Activision account.
