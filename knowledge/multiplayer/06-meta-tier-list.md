## What the tiers mean
- **META** = the strongest picks right now
- **A** = very strong
- **B** = good, but situational
- **C / D** = weaker; usually secondaries and launchers

## Simple takeaways
- **Close range:** ISO Nightshade or VEL 46 (both unlock early — level 4 and 13).
- **All-round:** Axion is #1, but it unlocks late (level 52). Until then, use the **M4** (level 14).
- **Long range / one-shot:** Mosin-Nagant is top marksman; KG-7 Vulcan is the top sniper and unlocks at level 4.
- **Shotgun:** Rezi 12.
- LMGs were nerfed and sit in B tier.

## Perk tier list

| Tier | Perk 1 | Perk 2 | Perk 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **META** | Ghost, Quick Fix | Ninja, Blood Rush | — |
| **A** | Resupply, High Alert | Birdseye, Bomb Squad | Scavenger, Cold-Blooded, Combat Grid, Tracker |
| **B** | Overkill, Kill Chain | Hardline, Fast Hands | Battle Hardened, Lethal Reach |

**A safe default:** Quick Fix (or Ghost) + Blood Rush (or Ninja) + Scavenger (or Cold-Blooded / Combat Grid).
