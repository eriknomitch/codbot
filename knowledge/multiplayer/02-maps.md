# Multiplayer Maps

[← Back to index](../README.md)

**At launch:** 12 Core 6v6 maps, 3 Ground War maps (32v32), 4 Gunfight maps (2v2), and Kill Block (3v3 / 10v10).
**Added after launch:** Shipment (October 27).

Interactive Tac Maps for each core map are on the official site: `callofduty.com/guides/modernwarfare4/multiplayer-maps/launch/<map-name>`.

## Core 6v6 maps (12)

| Map | Where | What it's like |
|---|---|---|
| **Cachette** | France | Old wine warehouse and garage turned into a secret armory, near a rail terminal |
| **Coal** | Former Soviet mountains | Old coal plant, fallen smokestack, ore chutes, dirty river |
| **Lithium** | Mexico | Lithium refinery under attack, below the Sierra mountains |
| **Lotus** | South Korea | Fishing village after a beach landing: market, hostel, boathouse, gardens |
| **Mumbai** | India | Hideout in a packed neighborhood, Victorian market, rail line through the middle |
| **Munition** | North Korea | Military base with ammo depot, skybridge, crane, water tower, hangars |
| **Nautical** | South Korea | A burning ferry leaving a war zone at dawn |
| **Reactor U92** | South Korea | Nuclear reactor in meltdown, big round control room, boiling pool |
| **Rooftops** | New York | Snowy hotel rooftops: greenhouse, HVAC, skylights, pigeon coop |
| **Sentry** | Russia | Snowstorm attack on a base: garage, generator room, command center |
| **Silkworm** | South Korea | Busy shopping street: convenience store, gaming den, subway entrance |
| **Transit 213** | Western India | Overgrown lot full of old colorful buses, repair shop, gas station |

**Post-launch (Oct 27):** **Shipment** — the classic tiny map, now set at a Russian harbor. Great for leveling guns fast.

Beta maps were: Cachette, Lithium, Lotus, Rooftops, Silkworm, Transit 213.

## Ground War maps (32v32)

All three are set in **Hajin**, the same area as the DMZ map.

| Map | What it's like |
|---|---|
| **Imjin Farmland** | Open rice fields, rivers, farms, a big mill. Long sightlines, lots of vehicles. |
| **Wolves Stadium** | Dense city around a stadium. Tall buildings, tunnels, rooftops. |
| **Forest** | Wooded hill between the other two. Guard tower at the top, gas station, barracks. |

## Gunfight maps (2v2)

| Map | What it's like |
|---|---|
| **Cafe** | Paris courtyard. Fountain blocks the middle; climb the statue for a view. |
| **Library** | Wood-paneled library. Bookshelves and a raised center walkway. |
| **NYC** | Snowy downtown with a big stage in the middle. |
| **Smuggler** | Rail yard. Fight in, around, and on top of train cars. |
