# Operators, Traits, and Crafting

[← Back to index](../README.md)

## Active Duty Operators

- You start with **2 Operator slots** (Vault Edition: **3**). DMZ Level 15 adds 1 more.
- Operators **share** your cash, stash, and resources.
- Each Operator has **their own gear and traits**.
- **Tip:** keep a strong Operator for important runs and a cheap one for risky loot runs.

## Dog Tag ranks and Trait Points

- Each Operator's **Dog Tag** levels up with successful runs: **Steel → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Damascus → Onyx (up to Onyx III)**.
- That's **18 levels**, and each one gives **1 Trait Point**.
- Max is **18 Trait Points** per Operator.

## Traits

There are **66 traits** across **3 trees**:

| Tree | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Warfighter** | Combat: survival, equipment, offense |
| **Scout** | Recon, looting, intel, finding resources |
| **Survivalist** | Healing, recovery, staying alive under pressure |

Some traits help with vehicles (for example, hopping out to fix a flat tire).

## MIA (when your Operator dies)

- The Operator can't deploy until you **recover** them with DMZ Cash at the Operators station.
- **Cost goes up** with how many Trait Points they have.
- They come back with traits, but **without** the gear they died with.
- 3D Printer upgrades can **lower recovery costs**.

## Operator Prestige

- Requires **18 Trait Points** plus some gear and cash.
- Resets normal traits, but gives a **permanent Prestige Trait**.
- Up to **3 Prestige Traits** per Operator.

## Crafting: the 3D Printer (DMZ Level 2)

### Basic resources (4 types)
**Synthetics, Electronics, Chemicals, Metals.** They're broken down automatically when you loot and **don't use stash slots**.

### Ingredients
Special items you must **find and extract**. Examples by rarity:
- **Uncommon:** Rigid Synthetics, Reactive Metals, Detonators, Electronics, Control Units, Biomaterials
- **Rare:** Structural Alloy, CPU, Nitro Compounds, Binding Catalyst, Stock Rod, High-Tenacity Fibers, Printed Circuit Board, Nylon Cloth
- **Other loot guides track:** Power Regulators, Comms Modules, Robot Cores, Electronic Sensors, Gold Bars, Pristine Cores

### Starter recipes
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Bandage | 15 Synthetics + 15 Chemicals |
| 1-Plate Armor Vest | 25 Synthetics + 25 Metals |
| Small Backpack | 25 Synthetics + 25 Metals |
| 2-Plate Armor Vest | 50 Synthetics + 5 Reactive Metals |
| Medium Backpack | 50 Synthetics + 5 Rigid Synthetics |

**The Medium Backpack is a top early craft:** more space plus **Secure Slots** that keep items even if you die.

### More recipes unlock as you level
Tacticals (Lvl 4), Lethals (Lvl 7), Field Upgrades (Lvl 12), Fire Support / killstreaks (Lvl 28), Gas Mask, Self-Revive Kit, Parachute, and more. Some recipes are also found as loot (for example, the Recon Drone recipe from a DMZ Order, or a Plate Carrier recipe in a locked room).

### FOB upgrades from the printer
Bigger stash, higher bank limits, cheaper MIA recovery, larger Dog Tag Case, and new station abilities.
