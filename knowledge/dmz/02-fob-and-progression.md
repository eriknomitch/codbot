# The FOB and DMZ Progression

[← Back to index](../README.md)

## FOB stations

The **Forward Operating Base (FOB)** is your home base. It grows as you level up.

### Available from the start
| Station | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Operators (Active Duty)** | Pick an Operator, check status, upgrade traits, recover MIA Operators |
| **Stash / Loadout** | Store loot; set what you bring in |
| **DMZ Orders** | Your mission list (tutorial, story, challenges) |
| **Firing Range** | Test guns; targets from close range to 100m+ |
| **Survival Kits** | Open themed loot packs |
| **Deploy** | The helicopter that starts your run |

### Unlocked later
| Station | How to unlock | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **3D Printer** | DMZ Level 2 | Craft gear and permanent FOB upgrades |
| **Boss Board** | Complete your **2nd DMZ Order** (reported) | Buy intel on Lieutenants; info on Commanders |
| **Vendor** | DMZ Level 5 | Buy weapons and gear for DMZ Cash; stock rotates daily |
| **Gunsmith** | DMZ Level 10 | Buy attachments, build guns from **Weapon Manuals** you've extracted |
| **Dog Tag Case** | DMZ Level 20 | Show off dog tags from players you've killed |
| **Bounty Leaderboard** | (not officially listed) | Top bounty hunters |
| **Battle Pass & Store** | Season 1 | Next to Survival Kits |

## Stash basics
- Starts with **40 item slots and 12 weapon slots**.
- Shared by all your Active Duty Operators.
- **Crafting resources don't use stash slots.**
- Grows with DMZ levels and 3D Printer upgrades.
- With no backpack, you get a **Basic Pack with 8 slots**.

## How to get weapons in DMZ
1. Loot them during a run.
2. Buy from the **Vendor**.
3. Build them in the **Gunsmith** using a **Weapon Manual** (find and extract manuals in Hajin).
4. Any attachment you've unlocked in MP or DMZ can be bought in the Gunsmith.
5. **Free loadout:** a random low-rarity gun and basic supplies. Good for low-risk runs.

## Ways to earn DMZ XP
- Looting containers (one of the steadiest sources)
- DMZ Orders and Story Missions
- Dynamic Ops and Side Ops (trucks, supply drops, radio towers, UAV towers, SAM sites)
- Killing AI and players (players give more)
- Lieutenants and Commanders (big payouts)

**Failing to extract does not erase DMZ XP.**

## DMZ Orders (missions)
- Grouped in **pages**. Early pages teach the basics; later ones tell the story.
- Some pages let you **choose** which chapter to play.
- **Page 1 (tutorial):** *Gear Up* (equip gun, backpack, vest, medkit), *Crackshot* (hit 3 range targets), *First Op* (deploy in Dynamic Ops). Reward: Medkit, 2-Plate Vest, $20,000.
- **Counterfeit, Part 1** examples: clear a Police Station; open a safe and extract $5,000; hack a UAV tower in Deadtown. Reward: Recon Drone recipe, $20,000, Explosives Kit.
- Full lists: Game8 and Gamers Heroes (see [../sources.md](../sources.md)).

## Tips
- Unlock the **3D Printer** early (just reach Level 2).
- The **Medium Backpack** gives **Secure Slots**, which keep items even if you die. Craft one ASAP.
- Do your Orders. They're the most reliable way to level up and grow your stash.
