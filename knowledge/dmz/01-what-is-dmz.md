# What Is DMZ? (Basics)

[← Back to index](../README.md)

## In one sentence
DMZ is an **extraction shooter**: you drop into a big map, grab loot, do missions, fight AI soldiers and other players, and then **get out alive** to keep what you found.

## Key facts
| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Map | **Hajin Exclusion Zone** (Korean Peninsula) — see [03-hajin-map.md](03-hajin-map.md) |
| Players | Up to **20 squads of 1–3 players** per match (solo is possible) |
| Enemies | AI soldiers, drones, robot dogs, helicopters, bosses, **and** other players (PvE + PvP) |
| Home base | **FOB** (Forward Operating Base), where you prepare between runs |
| Progression | Own track, **DMZ Level 1–70**, plus per-Operator **traits** |
| Returns from | MWII (2022) DMZ, now much deeper |
| Early access | Oct 20, 2026 for digital pre-orders |

## The game loop (5 steps)

1. **Prepare at the FOB.** Pick an Operator, equip guns and gear, check your **DMZ Orders** (your mission list).
2. **Pick how to deploy:**
   - **Story Missions** — follow the DMZ story; set rewards.
   - **Dynamic Ops** — objectives that pop up in the match (destroy a target, secure an item, kill a Lieutenant). Each success drops a **Supply Drop** that gets better each time (Common → Uncommon → Rare).
   - **Free Roam** — no set mission; loot and explore.
3. **Explore Hajin.** Loot containers, complete objectives, fight.
4. **Exfil (extract).** Go to an exfil point (green smoke), call the helicopter, defend until it lands, and board. Other ways out: **Fulton balloon** or **flare**.
5. **Back at the FOB.** Move loot from your backpack to your **Stash**, upgrade traits, craft, and repeat.

## What happens if you die
- You lose the gear you brought and the loot you found (except items in **Secure Slots** on some backpacks).
- Your Operator goes **MIA** (Missing in Action). Pay DMZ Cash at the Operators station to recover them. They keep their **traits**, but lose their gear.
- **You still keep the DMZ XP** you earned that run.

## Danger meters to watch (on your HUD)
- **Threat Level** (under the compass): how dangerous your current area is. It rises when you fight loudly.
- **Hostiles Kill Count:** if it fills, an enemy **Hunt Squad** comes for you and you become **Marked for Death**.
- **Time Remaining:** a **radiation storm** spreads across Hajin over time. Leave before it gets you.
- **Bounty / Wanted:** kill 4 rival Operators and you become **Wanted**. Nearby players are alerted.

## Social tools
- **Proximity chat** to talk to nearby strangers.
- **Ping wheel** lets you request a **Truce** or join up with another squad.

## After Action Report
After every run you get a grade from **F to S++** based on Objectives, Combat, and Looting. It lists your loot, cash, XP, completed Orders, and Dog Tag progress. Aim for a balanced run (not just looting) to get a high grade.

## How DMZ links to other modes
- Weapon XP, camos, and attachments are shared with Multiplayer.
- Unlocking a gun or attachment in MP lets you **buy** it in the DMZ Gunsmith (you still pay DMZ Cash).
- Hajin also becomes a **Warzone** battle royale map in Season 1.
