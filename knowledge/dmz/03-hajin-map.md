# The Hajin Map

[← Back to index](../README.md)

**Hajin** is a large "Exclusion Zone" where North Korea, South Korea, and Russia meet. It has **13 major Points of Interest (POIs)** and **60+ named locations**.

**Simple rule:** the **south and east are calmer**; the **northwest and center are the most dangerous**.

The Tac-Map uses a grid: letters go west → east, numbers go north → south (e.g., **D7 = Imjin Farmland**). Keys you find list their grid square.

## Threat levels (lowest to highest)
**Low → Medium → High → Critical → Extreme**

This is the danger from **AI enemies before you start shooting**. It does **not** count other players. Higher threat = more and tougher enemies, but better loot.

## The 13 major POIs

| POI | Area | Threat (sub-areas) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **14th Political Prison** | Northwest (NK) | Prison **High**, Prison Yard **High**, The Citadel **Critical** | Walled prison with watchtowers. The Citadel is a maze of walkways and cells. |
| **Chang-san Air Base** | West (NK) | Exterior **Critical**, Bunker **Extreme** | Built into a granite hill. The underground bunker is bigger than the base. Best entry: woods to the south. |
| **Deadtown** | Southwest (NK) | Exterior **Low**, Re-Education Center **Medium** | Empty farm town. Sewers, clinic, 2 gas stations. |
| **Imjin Farmland** | South-southwest (SK) | Farmland **Low**, Jeong Loading Dock **Low**, Rice Mill **Medium** | Open rice fields. The Rice Mill gives high ground. |
| **Cheongun Village** | North (SK) | Village / Border / Outskirts **Low**, **Heavenly Luck Casino High** | Border town. The casino is dark; bring **night vision (NVGs)**. |
| **Mirae General Hospital** | North-central (SK) | **Low** | Big hospital with triage tents; close-quarters fights inside. |
| **Cheonwang Peak Array** | Central (SK) | **Medium** | TV station and big antenna on a summit; rooftop helipad. |
| **Haneul Nuclear Reactor** | Northeast (SK) | Reactor **High + radiation**, SK Border Zone **Medium** | Need a **gas mask and radiation gear**. Enter by water or the tunnel from Compound Delta. |
| **NuriGO Mall** | Northeast Hajin City (SK) | East **Medium**, West **High** | Huge underground mall; use NVGs. Robot dogs (K9s) underground. |
| **The Grid** | Central Hajin City (SK) | Grid **Critical**, **Nenshin Tech Complex Extreme**, DBN HQ / Hanex Bank / Minsok Art School / Construction Zone **Critical** | Walled-off downtown. Rooftop and street fights. Hard to escape. |
| **Songdo Wharf** | East Hajin City (SK) | All **Low** (Wharf, Oamsik Turtle Island, Shoreline Station) | Quiet waterfront. Good for beginners. |
| **Wolves Stadium** | South-central Hajin City (SK) | Stadium **Medium**, Theatre **Medium** | Ruined baseball stadium. The north edge (facing The Grid) is the most dangerous. |
| **Hajin River Heights** | Southeast (SK) | All **Medium** (incl. South Island, Jinseo Park Towers) | Apartments. Has **Compound Echo**. |

### Smaller places (minor POIs)
Hajin River, Tong-il Village, Revolution Dam (military police), Pyonghwa Village (pontoon bridge), Imjin Suburb, Mujin Pass (snipers reported), Daesong Ridge, Namjin Riverside.

## Getting around

- **Vehicles need fuel and repairs** in DMZ (not in Warzone).
- **Ground:** Camper, Sedan, Grizzly, Dreadnought (Aston Martin SUV), Polaris Dagor, LTV (can have a .50 cal), Cargo Truck, D50 Radar Flatbed (has a built-in UAV).
- **Rail (automated):** a **Train** through South Korean POIs and an elevated **Tram** loop around Hajin City.
- **Water:** RHIB boats.
- **Air:** Heli; Heavy Chopper (rarely player-flown). The **Monarch** handles infil/exfil.

## Map tips
- Locked from the inside? There's usually another way in.
- Look for **underground entrances** in rural and wooded areas.
- Some POIs have **hidden entrances only reachable by water**.
- The 3D Printer gives hints about where specific materials spawn.
