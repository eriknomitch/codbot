## DMZ gun tier list (PvP)

> **Source:** [wzstats.gg/mw4/dmz](https://wzstats.gg/mw4/dmz), updated **Oct 29, 2026**. The site also shows the DMZ Cash cost of each build and has filters by budget. It changes often, so check the live page.

The DMZ list is **different from Multiplayer**. DMZ favors steady, armor-shredding rifles.

| Tier | Guns |
|---|---|
| **META** | **Han 86** (AR #1), **M4** (AR #2), **ISO Nightshade** (SMG #1), **Kastov 762** (AR #3), **PPSh-41** (SMG #2) |
| **A** | Rezi 12, X-58 Nyx, Axion, Type 73, MAR-9, FiNN LMG, Signal 50, KG-7 Vulcan |
| **B** | Patriot XMR, Hyeon Burst, Oris 8.6, Cronen Squall |
| **C** | VEL 46, Wz.55 Striga, Krait P68 |
| **D** | .50 GS, Oden, Warlord, APOC-338, Mosin-Nagant, Astaroth .57, Sang 9mm, Mojave .357, all launchers, knife |

**Key differences from Multiplayer:**
- **Han 86** is #1 in DMZ but only A tier in MP.
- **Mosin-Nagant** and **Axion** are top in MP but lower in DMZ.
- **LMGs** (Type 73, FiNN) are stronger in DMZ.
- The **Han 86, M4, ISO Nightshade, Rezi 12, MAR-9, FiNN, and KG-7** unlock early, so they're cheap and easy to get.

## Beginner game plan

### Your first few runs
1. **Do the tutorial Orders** (Gear Up, Crackshot, First Op). They pay $20,000 and gear.
2. **Land somewhere Low threat:** Imjin Farmland, Songdo Wharf, Cheongun Village, or Mirae Hospital.
3. **Bring only what you need.** Use a **free loadout** for low-stakes looting runs.
4. **Open every container.** Looting is steady XP and crafting materials.
5. **Exfil early.** Getting out with a little beats dying with a lot.

### Next steps
6. Hit **DMZ Level 2** to unlock the **3D Printer**. Craft a **Medium Backpack** (Secure Slots) and **2-Plate Vest**.
7. **Clean out your backpack** into the stash after every run. Space is tight early.
8. Reach **Level 5 (Vendor)** and **Level 10 (Gunsmith)**. Start extracting **Weapon Manuals**.
9. Run **Dynamic Ops** back-to-back. Each success gives a better Supply Drop.
10. Use **two Operators**: a "main" and a cheap "runner" for risky trips.

### Staying alive
- **Stay quiet** in Medium+ areas: suppressors, melee, throwing knives.
- **Watch the Threat Level.** If it climbs, back off.
- **Bring a gas mask** for the Reactor and irradiated areas (check Fire Stations for radiation gear).
- **Bring NVGs** for the Casino, NuriGO underground mall, and bunkers.
- **Exfil sites are ambush spots.** Flares are loud and visible. Be ready to fight.
- **Don't go Wanted by accident.** Four player kills puts a target on you.
- **Lieutenant dog tags reveal you.** Grab and go.
- **Watch the radiation storm timer.**

### Making money (general advice from guides)
- Clear **Compounds** and **Police Stations** (armories).
- Open **safes** (often in compounds and locked rooms).
- Complete **Dynamic Ops** and **Side Ops** (UAV towers pay cash).
- Kill Lieutenants (cash even without the tag).
- Claim **player bounties**.
