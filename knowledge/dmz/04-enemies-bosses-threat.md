## How threat works

- Each area has a starting **Threat Level** (Low → Extreme), shown under your compass.
- **Fighting loudly raises it** and brings bigger reinforcements.
- **To lower it:** break line of sight, hide, move away, go to a rural area, or play quietly.
- **Go quiet:** melee kills, throwing knives, and suppressed guns help you avoid detection.
- **Hostiles Kill Count:** kill too many AI and a **Hunt Squad** comes after you ("Marked for Death").

## Bosses

There are **13 bosses at launch: 3 Commanders and 10 Lieutenants** (reported by several guides).

### Commanders (roaming, very tough)
They move around Hajin. Many normal weapons barely hurt them; you need tactics and heavy firepower.

| Commander | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Colossus** | Heavy armored soldier with a **minigun** |
| **Deathstalker** | Attack helicopter (captured **SAM sites** help a lot) |
| **Main NK Battle Tank** | A tank that patrols the streets |

**Reward:** prized **3D Printer ingredients** (reported as Legendary), big DMZ XP and Cash, and Boss Board progress. Commander info on the Boss Board is reported to be **free**.

### Lieutenants (fixed areas, elite)
- Tougher than normal soldiers, with their own fighting style and escorts.
- **How to find one:**
  1. Buy intel at the **Boss Board** (reported **$25,000**, lasts one deployment), or
  2. Climb a **Lieutenant Hunt tower**, or
  3. Climb a damaged **radio tower** (Side Op) for a rough location.
- **Rewards:** DMZ XP and Cash just for the kill, their **weapon** (not guaranteed), a chance at a **Key** to their private loot room, and their **Dog Tag Case**.
- **Warning:** picking up the Dog Tag Case **shows your location to every player on the map**. Exfil fast. You can leave it, but you need it to progress the Boss Board.

## Player bounties (PvP)

- Killing rival Operators raises your **bounty**, shown on your **Dog Tag**.
- **4 Operator kills = Wanted.** Nearby players are alerted.
- Hunters can buy intel at **Bounty Stations** with DMZ Cash, kill you, and **extract with your dog tag** for a payout. Higher bounty = bigger payout.
- You can **pay off your own bounty** at a Bounty Station.
- DMZ levels 30/40/50/70 raise your bounty payouts (+10% up to +100%).

## Dynamic Op warning
Taking the **"Hunt Operators"** Dynamic Op means you'll face a **full squad of real players**.
