## Reliability notes

- **Official** facts come from the Call of Duty blog and Activision patch notes. They are the most reliable.
- **Meta and tier lists** come from wzstats.gg. They change often (sometimes daily). Check the live page before relying on them.
- **Boss names and some DMZ details** come from fan guides (Game8, games.gg, and others). Treat them as "reported," not official.
- The game launched one week ago. Expect balance patches to change numbers.
