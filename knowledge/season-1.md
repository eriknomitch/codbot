## DMZ

- **New location: X01** — a former GRANIT Arms (Russian) black site taken over by North Korean forces at Hajin's northern border. Dark tunnels, cargo bays, underground labs, production lines, and a sealed **Deep Vault**. Exfils can close, so know your way out.
- **Ghost** guides you through new DMZ Orders.
- **New enemies:** the **100 Spirits** NK faction (more is classified).
- **New gear** found only in X01.
- **Boss Board additions:** Lieutenants **Tae-Hyun ("Knuckle")** and **Chol-Min ("The Wrench")**; Commanders **The Captain** (Juggernaut) and an **autonomous Wheelson**.
- **FOB:** new Battle Pass & Store station; BlackCell owners get extra Vendor stock.
- **Events:**
  - **Threat Briefing: Battle Tank** — take one tank part per run for growing rewards.
  - **Threat Briefing: The Captain** — collect his helmet pieces in X01.
  - **Resupply Run** — find items across POIs and trade them for Self Revives, 3-Plate Carriers, and more.

## Battle Pass
- **100 tiers**, linear track, mix of free and premium.
- **1,100 COD Points** (Bundle: 2,400).
- **New catch-up system:** premium owners who fall behind are caught up to the current day's reward (up to day 50). 50+ rewards guaranteed.
- Tier 100: "Bellum" Ultra Mosin-Nagant blueprint.
- **BlackCell** (included in Vault Edition): XERO Operator, 20 tier skips, 1,100 CP, exclusive skins and blueprints.
- Season 1 Operators: **Blix** (Battle Pass) and **XERO** (BlackCell).
